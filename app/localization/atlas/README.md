# SRI24 Multi-Channel Brain Atlas (TZO116+ Parcellation)

## Provenance and Space
- **Atlas Name**: SRI24 Multi-Channel Atlas of Normal Adult Human Brain
- **Parcellation**: TZO116+ (Tzourio-Mazoyer 116 cortical/subcortical regions plus additional detailed parcellation, total 421 regions)
- **Reference**: Rohlfing T, Zahr NM, Sullivan EV, Pfefferbaum A. The SRI24 multichannel atlas of normal adult human brain structure. Human Brain Mapping 2010; 31(5):798-819.
- **Space**: SRI24 template space. This matches the exact template space used by the BraTS (Brain Tumor Segmentation) 2021 cohort (240 x 240 x 155, 1 mm isotropic spacing, skull-stripped).
- **Coordinate System**: RAS+ world orientation with affine signs [-1, -1, 1, 1] on LPS array indices.
